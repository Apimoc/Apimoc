Every seller has a number in their head. It usually comes from a neighbor's sale, and it is usually from the wrong month.

## The first two weeks

A listing gets its best traffic in its first fourteen days. Price it high and plan to reduce, and you spend that traffic on a number nobody was going to pay.

The reduction does not reset the clock. Buyers see the price history.
