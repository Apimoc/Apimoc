In a competitive market, the offer that wins is usually not the highest one. It is the one that looks the most likely to close.

## Where offers fall apart

A seller who has been under contract once already and had it collapse is not looking for the biggest number. They are looking for the offer least likely to waste another six weeks.

> The question a seller is really asking is: will this close, and when.

Financing, timeline and contingencies say more about that than price does.
